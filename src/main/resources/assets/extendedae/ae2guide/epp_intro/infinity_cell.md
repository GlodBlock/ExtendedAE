# ME Infinity Cell

A convenient source and sink for unlimited water and cobblestone in storage cell form.

<Row>
<ItemImage id="extendedae:infinity_water_cell" scale="4"></ItemImage>
<ItemImage id="extendedae:infinity_cobblestone_cell" scale="4"></ItemImage>
</Row>

Each cell has a capacity of 2.1 billion units, and you can extract or insert unlimited amounts of cobblestone or water.
