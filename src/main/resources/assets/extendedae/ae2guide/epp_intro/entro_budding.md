# Budding Entroized Fluix

<GameScene zoom="4" background="transparent">
  <ImportStructure src="../structure/budding_entro.snbt"></ImportStructure>
  <IsometricCamera yaw="195" pitch="30"></IsometricCamera>
</GameScene>

Budding Entroized Fluix is the source of <ItemLink id="extendedae:entro_crystal" />s. You can obtain it by injecting an <ItemLink id="extendedae:entro_seed" /> into a <ItemLink id="ae2:fluix_block" />.

The growth mechanism of Entro Buds is similar to that of [Certus Quartz](ae2:items-blocks-machines/budding_certus.md). However, the degradation
of Budding Entroized Fluix is unavoidable, and it will eventually turn into a <ItemLink id="ae2:quartz_block" />.
Theoretically, you can obtain about 10 <ItemLink id="extendedae:entro_crystal" />s from a Fully Entroized Fluix without the Fortune enchantment.

Budding Entroized Fluix will always drop **one** <ItemLink id="extendedae:entro_dust" /> when broken, regardless of its state.
An Entro Bud will drop an <ItemLink id="extendedae:entro_shard" /> when it is not fully grown.
