# Circuit Slicer

<Row gap="20">
<BlockImage id="extendedae:circuit_cutter" scale="8"></BlockImage>
</Row>

Tired of the Inscriber's slow speed? Try the Circuit Slicer! It can slice material blocks directly into processor
[prints](ae2:items-blocks-machines/processors.md), making it up to nine times faster than an Inscriber in most cases.

**Notice: Glass faces without ports cannot connect to the network.**
