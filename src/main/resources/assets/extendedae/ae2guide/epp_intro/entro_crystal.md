# The Entro Crystal

<Row>
<ItemImage id="extendedae:entro_crystal" scale="4"></ItemImage>
</Row>

*"Entro Crystals exhibit an extremely strong dimensional affinity after Ender Dust is injected into Fluix Crystals, allowing them to resonate
across space-time."*

Entro Crystals are the main crafting ingredient for most ExtendedAE items and machines. Use an <ItemLink id="extendedae:entro_seed" /> to turn
a <ItemLink id="ae2:fluix_block" /> into [Entroized Fluix Budding](./entro_budding.md), then harvest an Entro Cluster to obtain Entro Crystals.
