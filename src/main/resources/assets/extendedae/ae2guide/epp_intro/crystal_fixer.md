# ME Crystal Fixer

<BlockImage id="extendedae:crystal_fixer" scale="8"></BlockImage>

The ME Crystal Fixer can repair a Budding Certus Quartz block and turn it into its upgraded form.

It requires a <ItemLink id="ae2:charged_certus_quartz_crystal" /> and power to operate. Right-click it with a <ItemLink id="ae2:charged_certus_quartz_crystal" /> to insert the crystal.
