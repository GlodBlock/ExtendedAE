# ME Pulverizer

<Row>
<BlockImage id="extendedae:pulverizer" p:working="true" scale="8"></BlockImage>
</Row>

ME Pulverizer can break items down into small pieces. A common use is to grind crystals into dusts efficiently.

It also can process other items, like Blaze Rods, to obtain more products.
